How software freelancers price client work

Source-backed planning bands for websites, apps, APIs, design, QA, DevOps, maintenance and consulting. 30 service units · 18 cited sources · INR, excluding GST.

Read this first

India has no official freelance software tariff. Public fixed-price evidence is fragmented, definitions differ and many guides are seller-authored. These are broad planning bands, not official tariffs, guaranteed market prices or statistical percentiles.

Core rule: price simple content sites by page. Price functional software by workflows, features, integrations and delivery responsibility. A screen with permissions, payments, sync or offline state may cost more than ten static screens.

Low = credible lower end for clear, contained scope. Mid = planning anchor, not a measured median. High = upper planning bound before agency or enterprise premiums. USD sources are context only; INR bands are triangulated rather than mechanically converted.

Pricing models

  • hourly/day rate: uncertain scope, consulting, debugging, ongoing engineering. Watch: client carries effort risk; require time cap and reporting
  • fixed project: clear deliverables and acceptance criteria. Watch: change requests and hidden assumptions cause disputes
  • milestone fixed fee: multi-stage builds. Watch: define acceptance, dependencies and payment release per milestone
  • retainer: maintenance, fractional engineering, reserved capacity. Watch: state included hours, rollover, SLA and overage rate
  • value-based: measurable high-value outcomes. Watch: hard to benchmark; still define scope, assumptions and change control

Scope before quoting

Common inclusions

discovery call and written scope, implementation named in scope, basic responsive behavior where relevant, developer testing, one deployment/handover, limited defect warranty.

Usually excluded

GST/taxes, domain, hosting and cloud usage, paid themes/plugins/fonts/assets, app-store accounts and fees, third-party API/SMS/email/payment usage, copywriting/content entry beyond stated amount, hardware/devices, formal penetration test or compliance certification, major data cleanup/migration, ongoing support beyond warranty, scope changes.

Quote drivers: number and novelty of workflows, quality/completeness of requirements, custom design vs template, content/data readiness, roles and permissions, integrations and API quality, payments, maps, media, real-time or offline features, security/privacy/compliance, browser/device/platform matrix, migration and legacy constraints, test depth and acceptance criteria, performance/availability targets, deployment environments and observability, timeline/urgency and meeting load, warranty, SLA and ongoing support, IP/source-code handover and documentation, client market, procurement burden and payment risk.

Write down deliverables, acceptance criteria, dependencies, revision limits, change control, IP handover, milestones, warranty and support. For retainers, state included hours, rollover, SLA and overage.

Evidence limits

  • India has no official freelance software tariff; public evidence is fragmented and often seller-authored.
  • Fixed-price guides often mix freelancer and agency prices and omit scope.
  • Marketplace ranges are global, not India-only, but are useful for cross-border ceilings and role comparison.
  • Per-page pricing works mainly for marketing sites; functional pages should be quoted as workflows or estimated effort.
  • Low-confidence categories have especially thin public India-specific transaction data.
  • Validate any live quote against a written scope and at least two comparable freelancers.
